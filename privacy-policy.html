Vardora — Privacy Policy

Developer: Faryah Mobile  |  Effective date: August 16, 2026  |  App: Vardora (com.faryahmobile.vardora)

Vardora is a shift calendar app that runs entirely on your device. This policy describes exactly how the app handles your data — nothing here is boilerplate; it is written to match the app's actual code.

Summary

  • No personal data is collected, transmitted to a server, or shared with third parties.
  • The app never asks you to create an account; it does not collect your email, phone number, or identity.
  • Everything you enter (shift types, patterns, calendar exceptions, settings) is stored only in your device's local storage.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • No internet connection is required; the app works fully offline.

What data is processed?

The shift types, shift patterns (cycles), calendar exceptions (leave/sick days, notes) and reminder preferences (on/off, time) you enter into Vardora are stored in a private SQLite database on your device. This data:

  • Stays on your device only — it is never sent to Faryah Mobile's servers or anywhere else.
  • Remains on your device until you delete the app; uninstalling automatically erases it via your operating system.
  • Can optionally be exported to a file from Settings > Backup, to a location you choose (your own Google Drive, device storage, etc.) — this export never sends any data to Faryah Mobile.

Permissions

Vardora only requests the following permissions, each tied directly to a core feature:

  • Notification permission: to show shift reminders.
  • Exact alarm permission: so the reminder arrives at the exact time you chose.

No camera, location, contacts, files, or microphone permissions are requested.

Third-party sharing

Vardora does not share, sell, or use your data for advertising with any third party. The app contains no ad network, analytics library, or tracking SDK.

In-app purchases

When you purchase Vardora Pro, the transaction is processed entirely through Google Play's billing system. Faryah Mobile never has access to your payment card details; it only stores the purchase status (active/inactive) reported by Google Play, on your device.

Data security and deletion

Your data is protected by Android's app sandboxing (only Vardora can access it). To delete all your data, simply uninstall the app — this permanently erases all local data.

Children's privacy

Vardora is not directed at children and does not target users under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data behavior changes, this page will be updated and the effective date revised. Material changes will be noted in the app's update notes.
