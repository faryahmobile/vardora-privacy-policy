Vardora — Privacy Policy

Developer: Faryah Mobile  |  Effective date: August 31, 2026  |  App: Vardora (com.faryahmobile.vardora)

Vardora is a shift calendar app that runs largely on your device. This policy describes exactly how the app handles your data — nothing here is boilerplate; it is written to match the app's actual code.

Summary

  • No personal data (your name, email, or identity) is ever collected or shared with third parties for their own purposes.
  • The app never asks you to create an account.
  • Everything you enter (shift types, patterns, calendar exceptions, settings) is stored only in your device's local storage and never leaves it.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.
  • A limited, anonymous network connection is used only to prevent abuse of the free trial (uninstalling and reinstalling to renew it indefinitely) and to process Vardora Pro purchases — see "Trial abuse prevention" below.

What data is processed?

The shift types, shift patterns (cycles), calendar exceptions (leave/sick days, notes) and reminder preferences (on/off, time) you enter into Vardora are stored in a private SQLite database on your device. This data:

  • Stays on your device only — it is never sent to Faryah Mobile's servers or anywhere else.
  • Remains on your device until you delete the app; uninstalling automatically erases it via your operating system.
  • Can optionally be exported to a file from Settings > Backup, to a location you choose (your own Google Drive, device storage, etc.) — this export never sends any data to Faryah Mobile.

Trial abuse prevention

When your free trial starts, a one-way (SHA-256) digest of your device's hardware identifier (Android ID) is computed and stored on Google Firebase infrastructure. The original device identifier cannot be recovered from this digest. Its only purpose is to detect whether this device has already used the free trial, so the trial cannot be renewed indefinitely by uninstalling and reinstalling the app. This data is never linked to your name, email, or location, is never used for advertising, and Google does not use it for its own purposes — Firebase acts solely as our infrastructure provider (data processor). If no network connection is available, this check is simply skipped and retried later; it never blocks the app's core, offline functionality.

Permissions

Vardora only requests the following permissions, each tied directly to a core feature:

  • Notification permission: to show shift reminders.
  • Exact alarm permission: so the reminder arrives at the exact time you chose.

No camera, location, contacts, files, or microphone permissions are requested. The app does declare the standard, invisible Internet permission (required for Google Play billing and the trial abuse prevention described above); it never triggers a user-facing consent prompt.

Third-party sharing

Vardora does not share, sell, or use your data for advertising with any third party. The app contains no ad network, analytics library, or tracking SDK. Google Firebase and Google Play are used strictly as infrastructure/payment processors on our behalf, never for their own purposes.

In-app purchases

When you purchase Vardora Pro, the transaction is processed entirely through Google Play's billing system. Faryah Mobile never has access to your payment card details; it only stores the purchase status (active/inactive) reported by Google Play, on your device.

Data security and deletion

Your data is protected by Android's app sandboxing (only Vardora can access it). To delete all your data, simply uninstall the app — this permanently erases all local data. The irreversible device-identifier digest stored for trial abuse prevention is not personal data and can never be linked back to you.

Children's privacy

Vardora is not directed at children and does not target users under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data behavior changes, this page will be updated and the effective date revised. Material changes will be noted in the app's update notes.
